About the trading bot

Short-term swing strategy for learning and paper testing. Not investment advice; no real broker orders are submitted.

Market data: {{ provider_name }}.
During market hours: Yahoo Finance daily history plus a freshness-checked intraday quote is used for scan decisions; it can be delayed, incomplete or rate-limited. After market close: the scheduler attempts to capture the NSE website quote for every active position at 15:45 IST and saves it locally. The dashboard uses that saved value while the market is closed and switches back to live-quote mode during the next session.
Important: NSE’s public website endpoint is not a guaranteed or formally supported market-data API. If a symbol fetch fails, its last saved value is retained; first-time fallback prices are labelled. Google Search results are not used as a price feed because snippets can be stale or ambiguous.
Universe: full NSE regular EQ-series equity list, excluding non-EQ series such as ETFs, debt and SME listings.
Entry rules — all required
  1. Symbol is in the eligible NSE EQ list, not already active, and not in cooldown.
  2. Price is between ₹{{ min_price }} and ₹{{ max_price }}.
  3. Price is in the upper 40% of its 52-week range (range score at least 60%).
  4. A fresh quote/bar is available; stale daily close alone is not used for entry decisions.
  5. Prior 20-session average traded value meets the configured threshold (default ₹2 crore) and average volume meets the configured minimum (default 10,000 shares).
  6. Current quote breaks above the highest high of the previous 20 completed daily candles.
  7. EMA(9) > EMA(20), and the last completed daily close is above EMA(20).
  8. RSI(14) is between {{ rsi_min }} and {{ rsi_max }}.
  9. Latest completed daily volume is at least 1.2× the average of the preceding 20 completed sessions.
  10. Optional Gemini/news screening may add context; if unavailable or inconclusive, technical rules remain the basis.
  11. Limits: max {{ max_open }} open positions and {{ max_buys }} new paper buys per scan.
SOP risk controls and exits
  • Position size is calculated from configured paper-trading capital and risk-per-trade, capped by deployable capital; trades below the minimum size required for staged exits are rejected.
  • The initial stop is ATR/support-based by default, or fixed-percent when SOP_STOP_MODEL=FIXED. The stop and planned targets are stored with the active position.
  • Stop breach or an emergency technical trigger can close the full position. Emergency checks include bearish RSI divergence, RSI exhaustion reversal, bearish MACD cross, and a high-volume distribution candle.
  • After the first target (normally around +9% under fixed mode or a stop-distance-based target under ATR mode), the bot books 50% when position size permits and moves the stop to breakeven.
  • A later round-number rejection can trigger another partial sell; the remaining runner is managed using the SOP trailing-exit rule based on completed daily close versus EMA(9)/EMA(20) or the prior-session low.
  • A maximum holding period of 15 trading sessions triggers a time exit.
This is paper-trading logic, not a guaranteed loss limit. Gaps, slippage, delayed quotes, data outages and unfilled real orders can produce different results.
Strict SOP entry gates

Every entry must pass the technical gates and the external-checklist gates in the code. The technical gate includes price above the 30-week SMA; price > EMA(9) > EMA(20) > SMA(50); rising EMA slopes; RSI 55–68; bullish expanding MACD; a Bollinger squeeze/expansion; rising price structure; valid ATR; and one of two approved setups.

  • Setup A: compressed 10–30 session shelf/base breakout, breakout-day volume confirmation (at least 2× the 20-session average), acceptable trigger candle/reversal, and entry between 10:00 and 14:30 IST.
  • Setup B: post-spike low-volume pullback/retest near rising EMA(9) or shelf support with a green intraday reversal, also within the entry time window.
  • External company/NSE checklist data is fail-closed: missing or failed required checks block the entry. News/fundamental screening can also block an entry when strict confirmation is enabled.

These gates are intentionally selective; a stock can be skipped even when its price is rising. The rules are a mechanical implementation of the project SOP and are not a promise of profitability.

How scanning works
  • The symbol list refreshes from an official NSE equity-list CSV and keeps a cached list if NSE is temporarily unavailable.
  • MAX_SCAN_SYMBOLS controls the batch size, not the universe size. A saved cursor rotates through the full list across scans.
  • Scans run every {{ scan_interval }} minutes during market hours (India time), excluding dates listed in NSE_HOLIDAYS. Active positions are checked separately for exits.
  • Completed daily candles drive indicators; a fresh intraday quote is used for live breakout/exit decisions. Yahoo data may be delayed, incomplete or rate-limited.
  • Post-close prices for active positions are stored in data/closing_prices.json with source and timestamp. They are display/P&L snapshots, not new entry signals; live quote mode resumes during market hours. The 15:45 IST job runs on the scheduler host, so the bot process must remain running.
  • Post-close capture uses NSE website quote data as a best-effort source, not a contractual exchange feed. A failed request does not erase the last saved value; check the source/timestamp shown beside each price.
  • Trades are recorded locally as paper trades in CSV files. No broker orders are submitted.

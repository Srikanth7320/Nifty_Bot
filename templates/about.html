About the trading bot

Short-term swing strategy for learning and paper testing. Not investment advice; no real broker orders are submitted.

Data provider: {{ provider_name }}.
When the Upstox token is blank, Yahoo Finance is selected. When a token is configured, Upstox is selected. If Upstox cannot provide a usable quote, the symbol is skipped rather than traded on stale data.
Universe: full NSE regular EQ-series equity list, excluding non-EQ series such as ETFs, debt and SME listings.
Entry rules — all required
  1. Symbol is in the eligible NSE EQ list, not already active, and not in cooldown.
  2. Price is between ₹{{ min_price }} and ₹{{ max_price }}.
  3. Price is in the upper 40% of its 52-week range (range score at least 60%).
  4. A fresh quote/bar is available; stale daily close alone is not used for entry decisions.
  5. Prior 20-session average traded value meets the configured threshold (default ₹2 crore) and average volume meets the configured minimum (default 10,000 shares).
  6. Current quote breaks above the highest high of the previous 20 completed daily candles.
  7. EMA(9) > EMA(20), and the last completed daily close is above EMA(20).
  8. RSI(14) is between {{ rsi_min }} and {{ rsi_max }}.
  9. Latest completed daily volume is at least 1.2× the average of the preceding 20 completed sessions.
  10. Optional Gemini/news screening may add context; if unavailable or inconclusive, technical rules remain the basis.
  11. Limits: max {{ max_open }} open positions and {{ max_buys }} new paper buys per scan.
Exit rules — any one can trigger a paper sell
  • Price falls below EMA(9).
  • RSI falls below {{ rsi_exit }}.
  • Price falls below the high-water mark since entry minus 2 × ATR(14) captured at entry.
  • Maximum holding period of five trading sessions is reached.
No fixed percentage stop-loss or take-profit is used. ATR trailing exits are not a guaranteed loss limit; gaps, slippage, delayed quotes or outages can cause larger losses in real trading.
How scanning works
  • The symbol list refreshes from an official NSE equity-list CSV and keeps a cached list if NSE is temporarily unavailable.
  • MAX_SCAN_SYMBOLS controls the batch size, not the universe size. A saved cursor rotates through the full list across scans.
  • Scans run every {{ scan_interval }} minutes during market hours (India time), excluding dates listed in NSE_HOLIDAYS. Active positions are checked separately for exits.
  • Completed daily candles drive indicators; an intraday quote is used for live breakout/exit decisions. Yahoo data may be delayed, incomplete or rate-limited.
  • Trades are recorded locally as paper trades in CSV files. No broker orders are submitted.
